Read this with the gaps in mind. Tuned ACC and SAC save about as much as our MPC against the recorded cars, but only by following 50–59 m back instead of 40 m. The MPC is the only controller that saves energy at the recorded cars' own gap (41.3 m vs 40.0 m).

Battery model: BMW i3 physics, regen efficiency 0.65, 35 kW regen cap; energy is KE-adjusted Wh/km; windows are 120 s of real driving from days the controllers never saw (160 test windows).
