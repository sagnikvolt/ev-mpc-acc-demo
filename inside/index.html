Battery-aware cruise control, inside out.

A cruise controller that plans around the battery, tested on real traffic.Select a point to begin.

Illustrative concept car, not the BMW i3 our battery model is fitted to.

Paint and Wheels are just for fun: the car's look does not change any result.

Result

−1.89% median change in battery energy against 79 recorded commercial-ACC cars; lower in 59 of them.

Data

203 recorded 120 s windows from 5 OpenACC campaigns (160 held out for testing), and 68 real BMW i3 trips for the battery model.

Controllers

Six on equal terms: tuned ACC, sliding-mode, two reinforcement-learning, our MPC, and an oracle as the ceiling.

Checks

A MATLAB R2024b re-write agrees with this website to 0.000% (ACC) and 0.002% (MPC) at most, with 0 solver failures in 20 runs.
